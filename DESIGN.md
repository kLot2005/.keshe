# Design System: Minimalist Editorial Light Theme

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Visual Authority & Era
Inspired by modern high-craft editorial portfolios (minimalist Japanese/Nordic tech aesthetic). High contrast, crisp typography, generous whitespace, pristine light backgrounds, rounded pill navigation, and monochrome accent structures.

## Color System
- **Canvas / Background:** `#fafafa` (soft off-white) / `#ffffff` (pure white card background)
- **Foreground Text:** `#0f172a` (deep slate black) / `#334155` (secondary body text)
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#e2e8f0` (subtle light gray border)
- **Accent Active Pill:** `#0f172a` (solid black active indicator) with `#ffffff` text
- **Status Green Indicator:** `#22c55e` (emerald online pulse)

## Typography
- **Primary Sans:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif (Headings, buttons, labels)
- **Serif Contrast:** `Newsreader` / `Playfair Display`, serif (Role subtitle contrast)
- **Monospace:** `JetBrains Mono`, monospace (Code tags, badges, metrics)

## Layout & Components
- **Top Floating Pill Navigation:** Centered floating bar with active tab pill highlight.
- **Hero Grid:** Split 2-column layout (Headline + Bio + Socials on Left, Anime Developer Illustration on Right).
- **Cards & Modals:** Crisp white card containers (`bg-white border border-slate-200 rounded-3xl shadow-sm`).
- **Pill Action Buttons:** Full rounded buttons (`rounded-full px-6 py-3 font-medium`).
